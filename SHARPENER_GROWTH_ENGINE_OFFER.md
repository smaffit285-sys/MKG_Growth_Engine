# Sharpener Growth Engine — Product Offer

The Sharpener Growth Engine is a standalone, white-label operating system for professional knife sharpeners. It connects lead capture, CRM, consumer and commercial quoting, service records, custom invoicing, scheduling, reviews, referrals, rewards, user-generated content, marketing, and business analytics.

## Customization promise

Every deployment is tailored to the subscribing sharpener. Graphics, layout, colors, typography, logos, app icon, navigation, terminology, written content, service catalogue, pricing, invoice templates, offers, forms, automations, and customer journeys can be customized to the user’s needs.

The product is therefore not presented as a rigid MKG clone. Miami Knife Guy is the founding implementation and proving ground; each sharpener receives an experience that can reflect their own brand, market, pricing, services, and operating model.

## Core modules

- Customer CRM and unified activity timeline
- Residential and individual customer path
- Restaurant and commercial account path
- Quotes, service records, and custom invoices
- Configurable product and service catalogue
- Booking, pickup, delivery, and follow-up workflows
- Website lead capture and chatbot integration
- Referrals, rewards, reviews, UGC, and proof management
- Content and marketing pipeline
- Revenue, outstanding balance, pipeline, and retention dashboards
- Mobile installability for Android, Apple, tablet, and desktop devices
- Role-based staff access, quality records, and technician training data

## Customer outcome

The Growth Engine helps sharpeners capture more inquiries, respond faster, raise average order value, build recurring commercial revenue, collect more reviews and referrals, reduce administrative work, and retain organized customer and operational data as the business grows.
